Why a contractor may consider 6x4

A 6x4 dump truck can be useful when the job requires site access, shorter turning space or mixed public-road and jobsite work. Contractors moving aggregates, soil or construction waste should compare route access and material density before choosing between 6x4 and 8x4.

Using confirmed product-page information carefully

The SAGMOTO X1S 6x4 product page on this website includes a supplied reference configuration. Buyers can review that page for model-level data, but final legal payload, availability, homologation, price and destination compliance must be confirmed in writing before order.

Costa Rica RFQ questions

Send material, loading equipment, route length, gradients, operating surface, destination port, quantity and body preference. If the truck will work for contractors across multiple sites, explain the heaviest material and most difficult route, not just the average job.

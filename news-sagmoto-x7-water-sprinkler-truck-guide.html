SAGMOTO X7 Water Sprinkler Truck: Guide for Road and Construction Projects

Water trucks are strong enquiry products when buyers clearly define tank volume and spray equipment.

10 September 2026 · Andy

Direct answer: For a water sprinkler truck quote, buyers should send tank volume, road service use, pump flow, spray equipment, destination and quantity.

Define the water truck job

Dust control, road watering, municipal cleaning and construction supply may require different pump and spray layouts.

Match tank volume to chassis

A larger tank is useful only when the chassis, legal payload and road conditions support it.

Request visible proof before shipment

Inspection and loading photos can show tank body, spray equipment and general vehicle condition before export.

Send these details for a configuration-based quotation
  • tank volume and daily operating target
  • front spray, rear spray or water cannon needs
  • pump flow and hose requirement
  • road condition and destination rules
  • quantity and delivery timing

Frequently asked questions

Is a water tanker the same as a sprinkler truck?

Many buyers use both names, but the quotation should clearly define pump and spray functions.

What is the first configuration detail?

Tank volume and spray function should be confirmed first because they define the upper body.

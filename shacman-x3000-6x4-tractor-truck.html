SHACMAN X3000 6x4 Tractor Truck for Sale

The SHACMAN X3000 6x4 tractor truck is a configurable tractor head for long-haul freight, regional logistics and heavier trailer work. The correct export specification depends on the trailer, cargo, target gross combination weight, route distance, gradients, road surface, fuel range and destination requirements. This page shows configuration ranges already published in the site's X3000 material; it does not describe one fixed truck. Buyers should send their operating conditions and order details so one available combination can be selected and confirmed before quotation.

Match the tractor to the trailer and route

Trailer interface

Provide trailer type, kingpin and fifth-wheel requirements, trailer height, cargo and load distribution. Coupling details must be checked before the truck is specified.

Long-haul route

Share one-way distance, highway share, gradients, altitude, average speed, fuel access and driver-shift plan. These inputs frame the available drivetrain and fuel-range discussion.

Difficult operations

Describe rough roads, low-speed site work, dust, heat or sustained mountain sections. A power figure alone cannot confirm suitability for the duty.

X3000 6x4 tractor truck FAQs

What must be confirmed for an X3000 6x4 tractor truck quotation?

Confirm the trailer type, cargo, target operating weight, route distance, gradients, road surface, daily mileage, destination country and port, steering position, emission requirement, quantity and preferred quotation basis.

Are the X3000 ranges on this page one complete specification?

No. They are configuration ranges already published in the site's X3000 information. One engine, transmission, axle, wheelbase, tire, fuel-tank and fifth-wheel combination must be selected and confirmed in writing before quotation.

What should a fleet buyer standardize before ordering?

Where operating conditions are similar, fleet buyers can ask to standardize the selected powertrain, axle ratio, tires, fifth wheel, fuel capacity, cab equipment, spare-parts package and inspection checklist, subject to configuration availability.

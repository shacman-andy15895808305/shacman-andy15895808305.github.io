Match the tractor to the trailer and route

Use the tractor truck selection guide to compare drive layouts. A 4x2 request requires a separate specification; do not transfer the 6x4 reference values to it.

Engine power is only one selection input

Give the loaded combination weight, trailer layout, gradients, altitude, cruise speed and daily distance. A numerically lower final-drive ratio changes the gearing relationship; it does not by itself prove lower fuel use or adequate starting performance. Review engine, AMT, final drive and tyre rolling radius together. Neither table entry guarantees a fuel-consumption figure.

Leaf springs or rear air suspension?

The source lists both arrangements under each design model. Confirm the suspension variant explicitly rather than relying on the model code alone. Discuss trailer coupling height, load distribution, road surface and access to suspension diagnostics and replacement parts before choosing an arrangement.

Confirm the coupling and inspection scope

Include fifth-wheel height and rating, kingpin size, trailer electrical and air connections, rear clearance and turning space in the technical review. Before shipment, check vehicle identity, engine and transmission labels, axle specification, suspension and the agreed documents. A photograph or a regional catalogue is not a substitute for the order technical sheet.

X5000 configuration questions

Are 440 PS and 480 PS specifications available for every destination?

No. These reference entries belong to a South Africa-oriented assembly programme. Confirm supply format, engine emissions and destination registration documents before treating either configuration as an orderable complete truck.

Does a 13-tonne axle mean a 13-tonne legal payload?

No. A component rating is not a legal vehicle payload. Tractor, trailer, cargo, fifth-wheel loading and local axle and gross-weight limits must be checked together.

Can the photos confirm the engine, suspension or emissions?

No. Photos show reference vehicles, not proof of the configuration in the comparison table. Confirm the vehicle identity, component labels and agreed technical sheet during inspection.

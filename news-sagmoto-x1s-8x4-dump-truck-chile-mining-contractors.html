SAGMOTO X1S 8x4 Dump Truck for Chile Mining Contractors

A heavy-duty buying guide for contractors comparing 8x4 dump trucks for mining support, quarry hauling and infrastructure work.

16 September 2026 · Andy · Sales Manager

Direct answer: Chile buyers considering a SAGMOTO X1S 8x4 dump truck should define the material, haul road, slope and loading equipment before quotation. Heavy-duty work needs a route-based configuration discussion.

Mining and quarry work requires clarity

Mining support can mean moving ore, waste rock, aggregate, road base or construction material. These materials affect dump body design and practical operating weight, so they should be described before price discussion.

Why 8x4 is a separate decision

An 8x4 dump truck may be discussed when the buyer needs more capacity than a 6x4 truck, but road access, turning area and loading equipment must support the larger vehicle.

RFQ checklist

Send material, daily work cycle, road condition, slope, loading equipment, preferred body style, destination port and quantity. If exact capacity is uncertain, send the job description first.

Frequently asked questions

Is 8x4 always better for mining?

No. It depends on access road, material, loading equipment and operating cost.

Can body reinforcement be discussed?

Yes. Reinforcement should be discussed according to material and loading method.

Can photos be checked before shipment?

Buyers may request body photos, chassis photos, cabin photos, nameplate photos and loading preparation images.

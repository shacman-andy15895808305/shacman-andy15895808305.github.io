SHACMAN 6x4 Dump Truck for Cote d Ivoire Quarry Work

A quarry and construction buyer guide focused on haul material, dump body matching, lifting system questions and export quotation inputs.

14 September 2026 · Andy · Sales Manager

Direct answer: Cote d Ivoire quarry buyers should match the dump truck to material density, haul road, loading method and body reinforcement. A larger body is not automatically a better quarry truck.

Quarry material decides the body discussion

Stone, gravel, laterite, soil and mixed construction material behave differently in a dump body. Dense or abrasive material may require stronger floor and sidewall discussion, while lighter material may focus more on volume and loading speed. Buyers should describe the material, average load and loading equipment before asking for final price.

Haul road and lifting system questions

Short quarry cycles, rough internal roads, public road transfer and steep gradients all affect the recommended configuration. The lifting system, tailgate, body shape and stability during unloading should be confirmed with the site conditions. If the site has uneven ground, unloading area and safety practices need attention.

6x4 layout and configuration limits

A SHACMAN 6x4 dump truck can be discussed for quarry and construction work, but payload, body size, engine, transmission, axle and tire details depend on final configuration and destination requirements. Buyers should avoid treating a reference photo as a fixed specification.

RFQ information for a quarry buyer

Send destination, port, quantity, material, loading equipment, haul distance, road condition, target payload, body preference, operating hours and inspection needs. If body thickness or dimensions are required by your project, include those requirements for confirmation.

Features

The integrated stamping forming cab frame, combined with robot automatic welding technology, enhances the cab's load-bearing capacity, stability, and other aspects.

The V-shaped grid design of the front cover optimizes the CFD flow field of the entire vehicle, significantly reducing wind resistance.

The cab uses polyester sound insulation materials and integrated foam floor mats to bring excellent NVH performance, which makes driving more focused.

Combined with integrated headlights with LED daytime running lights, the longer and brighter illumination distance can reach up to 500 meters, providing safer driving and pedestrian protection.

Adjustable seat handles keep the driver's hands in the appropriate position for a better driving experience, as well as a better sleeper. There is more space under the sleeper, adding two compartments to provide more storage space. Optional hydraulic or air seats, combined with four point air suspension shock absorbers in the cab, greatly enhance driving smoothness and ride comfort.

Equipped with 840mm wide warm color lining sleeper for a good resting experience.

The three-level hinged upper pedal adopts a human-machine design, with a larger distance between the pedals, making it more convenient and safe for the driver to get on and off the vehicle.

The dashboard adopts the form of LCD+mechanical pointer gauge, which allows for clearer understanding of vehicle information such as fuel level, water temperature, battery level, and oil pressure through the screen

SAGMOTO X3S Export Buying Guide

SAGMOTO X3S trucks are available in different vehicle types and configurations. The appropriate engine, transmission, axle, emission standard, tires and body specification must be confirmed against the destination country, payload requirement and working conditions.

Which information is needed for a quotation?

Provide the destination country, required truck type, quantity, payload, road or site conditions and preferred delivery time. A configuration and price can then be prepared for review.

Is one specification suitable for every market?

No. Emission requirements, climate, altitude, road conditions, fuel quality and local registration rules can affect the recommended configuration.

How is the export price determined?

Price depends on the confirmed configuration, emission standard, upper body or trailer, order quantity, destination and shipping method. Request a current quotation rather than relying on a fixed online price.

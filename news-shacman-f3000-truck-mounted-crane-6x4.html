SHACMAN F3000 6x4 Truck Mounted Crane: Buyer Checklist for Export Projects

Crane truck buyers need to match chassis, crane capacity and cargo body instead of selecting the crane alone.

10 September 2026 · Andy

Direct answer: A truck mounted crane quotation must combine chassis payload, crane lifting chart, boom type, outrigger layout, cargo body and destination registration rules.

Start with lifting radius, not only tonnage

A crane rated tonnage does not describe what it can lift at every distance. Send the working radius and typical cargo weight.

Check frame and outrigger layout

The subframe, outrigger span and mounting position must fit the chassis and the working site surface.

Confirm cargo body after crane selection

Crane position affects usable body length and payload. The cargo body should be quoted after the crane arrangement is clear.

Send these details for a configuration-based quotation
  • required lifting capacity and working radius
  • straight boom or folding boom preference
  • cargo body length and payload
  • jobsite ground condition and outriggers
  • destination, steering side and emission standard

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose the crane before the truck?

The crane and truck should be selected together because weight distribution and frame requirements affect safety and registration.

What documents should buyers request?

Ask for the chassis specification, crane model, lifting chart, body drawing and pre-shipment inspection photos.

What determines the recommended tractor specification?

Trailer and load

Trailer type, cargo, legal axle loads, target GCW and coupling requirements.

Route

Distance, gradients, altitude, road surface, average speed and refuelling conditions.

Market compliance

Emission standard, steering position, lighting, tires and registration documentation.

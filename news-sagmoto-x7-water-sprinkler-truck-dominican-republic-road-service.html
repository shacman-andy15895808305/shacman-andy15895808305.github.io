SAGMOTO X7 Water Sprinkler Truck for Dominican Republic Road Service

A buyer guide for road cleaning, construction watering, dust suppression and municipal support applications.

15 September 2026 · Andy · Sales Manager

Direct answer: Dominican Republic buyers should define whether the SAGMOTO X7 water sprinkler truck will mainly clean roads, control dust, carry water or support construction work. The pump and spray layout should follow that main task.

Road service is not only water transport

A road service water truck may need front spray, rear spray, side spray, hose connection or water cannon. The buyer should describe the road width, operating speed and refill distance before quotation.

Construction and municipal use

Construction support may require more attention to dust control and rough access roads. Municipal use may focus on regular street cleaning and controlled spraying around traffic. These use cases should not be treated as one fixed configuration.

RFQ checklist

Send country, destination port, quantity, target tank capacity, spray functions, water source distance, route condition and daily working hours. Final specification should be confirmed before quotation.

Frequently asked questions

Is the tank capacity fixed?

No. Tank capacity is available depending on configuration and route requirements.

Can one truck handle cleaning and dust control?

Often yes, but the spray system should be discussed around the main daily job.

Can shipment photos be provided?

Buyers can request truck, tank, pump, nozzle, nameplate, loading and shipping preparation photos.

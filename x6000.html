Features

Our X6000 features a spacious and intelligently designed cabin, ensuring every journey is a pleasure. With easy access to all vehicle information at your fingertips, you'll stay alert and comfortable on the road. The X6000 is engineered to provide a seamless and enjoyable driving experience.

Built on a brand-new SSA top-level vehicle body platform for enhanced protection. The body features an H-type load-bearing beam that increases the rigidity and stability. It also features a high-strength A-pillar that reinforces the roof structure and reduces the impact of rollover. Collapsible suspension technology is also employed to better absorbs collision energy. The vehicle fully meets the highest ECE R29 03 regulations.

Enjoy the quiet and comfort of our cab that features a fully enclosed design that seals out the noise and dust. The cab sealing performance that is 30% better than the industry average, which means a more pleasant and healthy driving environment. Our cab also has an idle noise level of only 49.8dB, which is comparable to a library.

Our cab also offers a range of other features that enhance the comfort and convenience of the driver and passengers including a foldable work table, 42 liter onboard refrigerator, 850mm soft sleeper with a high-quality mattress, bedside reading lamp, 1000 liter large storage space, 1.2KW inverter power supply and a electric sunroof.

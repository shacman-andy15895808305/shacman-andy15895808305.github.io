SHACMAN X3000 6x4 Dump Truck for Uzbekistan: Configuration Checklist

A practical checklist for selecting a SHACMAN X3000 6x4 dump truck in Uzbekistan: route, climate, material, 430 HP reference specs and inspection.

7 September 2026 · Andy Zhang

Direct answer: For Uzbekistan, an X3000 6x4 order should be matched to summer heat, winter starting, road-to-jobsite ratio, material density and local compliance. The 430 HP Weichai and FAST drivetrain is a documented reference, not a substitute for a country-specific specification.

Start with the duty cycle

Describe cargo, density, target payload, trips per day, paved-road percentage, jobsite surface and maximum grade. A short quarry cycle and a long road-haul cycle can require different axle ratios, tyres and body choices.

Reference powertrain

The available X3000 document lists a Weichai WP12.430E201 engine rated at 430 HP, 11.596 L and Euro III, paired with a FAST transmission. Confirm the exact transmission code, axle ratio and auxiliary braking in the final technical sheet.

Climate and maintenance planning

Share minimum and maximum operating temperatures and expected daily hours. Confirm fluids, batteries, cooling package, filtration, spare parts and service intervals for the intended region before shipment.

Pre-shipment control

The inspection list should follow the signed specification: cab, drivetrain tags, tyres, body dimensions, tools, documentation, labels and agreed options. Photos and inspection records should be tied to the vehicle identification.

Information to send for a precise proposal
  • destination country, port and quantity;
  • cargo, density and target legal payload;
  • road surface, daily distance and maximum grade;
  • steering, emissions, registration and other local requirements;
  • required delivery term and optional equipment.

Frequently asked questions

What horsepower is in the reference configuration?

430 HP.

Should the axle ratio be copied from another market?

No. It should match the Uzbek route and confirmed order.

What climate data is needed?

Expected minimum and maximum temperatures plus daily operating hours.

What is needed for a quote?

Destination, quantity, material, payload target, route, climate and required compliance.

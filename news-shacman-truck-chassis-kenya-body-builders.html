SHACMAN Truck Chassis for Kenya Body Builders

A chassis procurement guide for body builders planning mixer, tanker, cargo, crane and other special vehicle projects.

16 September 2026 · Andy · Sales Manager

Direct answer: Kenya buyers should start a SHACMAN truck chassis enquiry from the upper body application. A mixer, tanker, cargo body, crane or bulk cement body can require different wheelbase, PTO, frame and axle discussion.

Start from the body, not the chassis model

Body builders often ask for a chassis model first, but the better route is to describe the body. A water tanker, fuel tanker, mixer, cargo body and truck-mounted crane each place different loads on the frame and require different mounting discussions.

Data that reduces rework

If drawings or body dimensions are available, send them early. If not, describe the application, payload target, road condition and equipment mounted on the chassis. This helps Andy discuss feasible configurations without guessing.

Export RFQ checklist

Send upper body type, wheelbase target if known, road condition, steering side, emission requirement if known, quantity and destination. Final chassis data should be confirmed before quotation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy chassis without upper body?

It can be discussed depending on availability and destination requirements. Body builder information is still needed.

What if I do not know wheelbase?

Send the body application and size target first; wheelbase can be discussed from the application.

Can spare parts be quoted together?

Yes. A chassis order can include related maintenance parts or project spare parts if requested.

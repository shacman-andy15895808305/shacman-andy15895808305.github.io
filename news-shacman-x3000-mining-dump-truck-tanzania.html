SHACMAN X3000 Mining Truck Tanzania | Selection Guide

Select a SHACMAN X3000 dump truck for Tanzania mining and quarry work using route, material, body, tyres, filtration and support requirements.

7 September 2026 · Andy Zhang

Direct answer: An X3000 can be configured for mining or quarry support in Tanzania, but the correct truck cannot be selected from horsepower alone. Material density, haul distance, road surface, gradient, dust, ambient temperature, legal weights and maintenance capacity should drive the specification.

Map the haul cycle

Record loading point, dump point, one-way distance, elevation change, surface condition, queue time and trips per shift. This reveals whether the priority is traction, road speed, body durability, cooling or manoeuvrability.

Use verified data carefully

A documented X3000 6x4 reference uses a 430 HP Weichai WP12.430E201 Euro III engine and FAST transmission. The final Tanzania unit may require different emissions, ratios, tyres or protection; those items are confirmed per order.

Dust and body wear

Mining and quarry roads make filtration, radiator protection, tyre pattern and maintenance discipline especially important. State the material size and loading method so the body floor, side walls and protection can be reviewed.

Plan support before delivery

Prepare filters, routine service parts, tyre availability, driver instruction and inspection procedures before the trucks begin work. A parts list should reflect the exact engine, transmission and axles in the signed order.

Frequently asked questions

Is 430 HP always the best choice?

No. Power must be matched to gross weight, grade, speed and altitude.

What material data is needed?

Material type, density, maximum particle size and loading method.

Why provide route distance?

It affects gearing, cooling, fuel planning and productivity estimates.

Are prices shown online?

No. Configuration, freight, price and delivery are quoted per project.

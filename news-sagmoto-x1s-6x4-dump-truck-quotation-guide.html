SAGMOTO X1s 6x4 Dump Truck: Quotation Guide for Overseas Buyers

For Latin America and other non-European markets, the X1s 6x4 can be discussed as a practical dump truck option.

Define the job before the model

A 6x4 dump truck can work well for many construction and material transport tasks, but the route and legal payload must fit.

Confirm body style and volume

U-shape and rectangular bodies solve different loading and unloading problems. Body choice should follow material and operating habits.

Prepare language-ready materials

For Spanish-speaking buyers, product information and quotation notes should be clear enough for fleet partners and local registration review.

Send these details for a configuration-based quotation
  • country and port
  • material and payload target
  • 6x4 route condition and maximum grade
  • body shape and dimensions
  • Spanish or English specification requirement

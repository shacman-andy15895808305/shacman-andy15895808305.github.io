SAGMOTO X1S 8x4 Dump Truck for Dominican Republic Heavy Construction

A guide for buyers comparing heavier dump truck configurations for quarry, infrastructure and construction material work.

15 September 2026 · Andy · Sales Manager

Direct answer: Dominican Republic buyers should evaluate the SAGMOTO X1S 8x4 dump truck when heavier construction or quarry work requires more capacity than a smaller site truck can reasonably support.

Why 8x4 is a different buying decision

An 8x4 truck needs a suitable route, loading equipment and unloading area. It can be valuable for larger material movement, but the buyer should confirm whether roads and project access can support the complete vehicle.

Material and body planning

Quarry stone, road base, soil and demolition material can require different body shapes and reinforcement. The final body volume, steel discussion and hydraulic system should be confirmed before quotation.

How to prepare the enquiry

Send material type, road condition, loading equipment, daily cycle, destination port and quantity. If comparing 6x4 and 8x4, explain the current problem with your existing fleet.

Frequently asked questions

Is 8x4 always the best choice?

No. It depends on payload needs, road access, loading equipment and operating cost.

Can I use this as a quarry truck?

It can be discussed for quarry support, but final configuration should match material density and haul road.

What photos can be provided before shipping?

Finished truck, dump body, chassis, tire, cabin, nameplate and loading photos may be requested.

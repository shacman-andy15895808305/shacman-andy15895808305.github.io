SHACMAN Maintenance Parts Checklist for Africa Fleets

A practical parts enquiry guide for fleets that need filters, maintenance items and replacement parts for working trucks.

16 September 2026 · Andy · Sales Manager

Direct answer: Africa fleet buyers should send model, VIN, part photos, quantity and destination before requesting SHACMAN maintenance parts. Accurate identification is more important than asking for a general spare parts price.

Why parts enquiries need vehicle data

Different truck models and production configurations can use different filters, brake parts, chassis parts and engine-related components. A clear enquiry reduces the risk of wrong parts being quoted or shipped.

Build a small stock plan

For fleets working in mining, construction or long-distance transport, it is useful to group oil filters, fuel filters, air filters and other consumables by vehicle model. Buyers can also separate urgent repair items from regular stock items.

RFQ checklist

Send truck model, VIN if available, part name, part photo, old part number if visible, quantity, destination country and preferred shipping urgency. Final availability and part matching should be confirmed before quotation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I send only a part photo?

A photo helps, but truck model and VIN are better for matching.

Can maintenance parts be ordered with trucks?

Yes. Buyers can request a starting spare-parts package with truck procurement.

Do you publish fixed part prices?

No. Price and availability depend on part matching, quantity and shipping requirements.

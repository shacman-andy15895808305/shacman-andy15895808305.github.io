How to evaluate an export enquiry partner

For a useful comparison, ask for the destination-market configuration logic rather than relying on a generic model name. A suitable discussion starts with the work to be done: cargo or medium, expected payload, road and gradient conditions, steering position, local emissions requirements and required body or equipment.

For specialised trucks, the chassis and body must be considered together. A tanker, mixer, hook-lift, dump body or cargo body may require different wheelbase, power-take-off, frame layout, axle loading and accessory placement. Final specifications, availability and destination-market compliance must be confirmed in the agreed order documents.

Evidence a buyer can request

Where available for the specific order, buyers can request relevant vehicle inspection, loading and shipping photos. Use them to review visible vehicle condition and the loading sequence, while treating final technical documentation, agreed inspection requirements and destination-market compliance as separate checks.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the official SHACMAN or SAGMOTO website?

No. It is an independent export-sales information website operated by SHAANI XI FENGHAN TRADING CO.,LTD. Final model availability, specifications and destination-market requirements are confirmed for each order.

What should an overseas buyer provide?

Destination, intended application, cargo or payload, road conditions, quantity, steering position and emission requirements.

What proof can be requested before shipping?

Relevant vehicle inspection, loading and shipping photos can be requested where available. They support a buyer review and do not replace final agreed technical documents, inspection requirements or destination-market checks.

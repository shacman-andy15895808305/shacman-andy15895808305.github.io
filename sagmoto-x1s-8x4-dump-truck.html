8x4 selection for heavy construction and haul work

Material and body matching

Give the material type and approximate density, loading method and desired body. The complete body and chassis weight must be reviewed before a payload or operating limit is discussed.

Mine and construction roads

Describe gradients, loose or compacted surfaces, potholes, water, dust and the proportion of public-road travel. These conditions affect configuration review and operating planning.

Cycle and tipping conditions

Provide haul distance, trips per shift, loading time, unloading area, overhead clearance and ground stability. A stable tipping location is an operational requirement.

Why body and vehicle weight must be reviewed together

An 8x4 chassis does not create one universal payload figure. The body material, dimensions, floor and side thickness, hydraulic system, optional equipment and fuel all contribute to vehicle weight. The transported material and destination axle limits then affect the permissible operating plan. For an accurate quotation, the body request should be reviewed with the full chassis configuration rather than specified independently.
